# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Four overlapping audiences:

- **Book and newsletter readers**: people in tech teams (senior engineers, scientists, product and design people, tech leads, early managers) discovering *The Leadership Fork* and its biweekly newsletter. This is the primary audience; the main call to action is the newsletter signup.
- **Coaching clients**: leaders and managers looking for a coach who has led teams at scale.
- **Professional network**: former Amazon colleagues, industry peers and conference contacts.
- **Opportunities**: people evaluating Michael for speaking, advisory or board roles.

## Product Purpose

Personal homepage for Michael Donoser, Ph.D.: author of *The Leadership Fork* and leadership coach, former Director of Science at Amazon (contract ended September 2026). It is the canonical record of his professional identity and the source of truth for career facts (the CV and the book site follow it). Success is a visitor quickly forming an accurate, credible impression, subscribing to the newsletter, or finding the proof point or link they came for (CV, book, writing, contact channels).

## Positioning

Two tracks that meet in one question. Track 1, research: M.Sc. and Ph.D. in computer vision at Graz University of Technology, then Assistant Professor and tenured Senior Researcher (50+ peer-reviewed papers, 26 at the big five venues, 3,310 citations, h-index 27). Track 2, industry: twelve years at Amazon turning research into products, from founding the computer vision team in Berlin to Director of Science leading a cross-functional organization of up to 40 people, with 20+ launches and customer impact in the billions. Both tracks led to the same conclusion: the biggest leverage point in any organization is the people. That is what the book is about.

Book hook, shared with theleadershipfork.com: "Talent is rarely the problem. Habits are."

## Operating Context

Single-page static site (`index.html` with inline styles and script), no build step, hosted on GitHub Pages at mike-dono-0815.github.io/homepage. Sections top to bottom: Hero (with book card and newsletter call to action), Why a book (newsletter signup), Two tracks (career timeline), Leadership and coaching (six themes), Book, Writing, Personal projects, Contact. `imprint.html` and `privacy.html` still use the older `style.css`. The CV PDF is built from the separate `curriculum-vitae` repo (XeLaTeX) and copied here.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Plain HTML/CSS/JS only: no framework, no build tooling, no package.json.
- All content is hand-authored in `index.html`; no CMS.
- SEO and sharing metadata (Open Graph, Twitter Card, JSON-LD Person) must stay accurate when identity facts change. Michael is always "former" Director of Science, never current.
- External profiles (LinkedIn, Substack, Medium, GitHub, Google Scholar) are the full record; the site shows curated highlights.
- The book (*The Leadership Fork: 55 Mechanisms for Leading When the Playbook Stops Working*) comes out in 2027. Chapter 1 is the newsletter welcome gift. Do not imply a release date beyond "2027".
- Google's site name comes from the root repo mike-dono-0815.github.io, not from this site.

## Brand Commitments

- Name and credential: "Michael Donoser, Ph.D.", "Author and leadership coach", "former Director of Science at Amazon".
- American English. "Twelve years" at Amazon, never "more than a decade".
- Voice: direct, candid, substance first; concrete mechanisms over motivational abstraction.
- Visual identity: dark warm background (#131209), off-white text (#F5F5F0), muted (#A8A8A0), gold accent (#E8AC20); Geist and Geist Mono. The CV uses the same palette and fonts.

## Evidence on Hand

All real and already on the page; nothing may be invented:

- Career timeline 1996 to 2026 (Graz University of Technology, Amazon Berlin and Germany).
- Research record: 50+ peer-reviewed papers, 26 at the big five venues, 3,310 citations, h-index 27; four best paper awards (ICPR 2008, OAGM 2009 and 2010, ACCV 2012) and the Josef Krainer Prize 2008.
- Industry record: 20+ launches, customer impact in the billions, organization of up to 40 people.
- Book cover, chapter list, link to theleadershipfork.com and the newsletter.
- Writing on Substack, LinkedIn and Medium; personal side projects on GitHub with live demos.
- CV PDF, profile photo, contact links.

## Product Principles

1. The book and newsletter lead; career and coaching provide the proof and the second path.
2. Curated over exhaustive: highlights here, full records on the external profiles.
3. Every claim stays traceable to real evidence; no invented metrics, testimonials or dates.
4. Keep the three sources consistent: this homepage, the CV and theleadershipfork.com.
